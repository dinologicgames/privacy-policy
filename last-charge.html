Last Charge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Last Charge (the "Game") is a mobile puzzle-platformer for Android published by Dino Logic Games ("we", "us"), based in Kraków, Poland. This policy explains what information the Game collects, how it is used, and how parents and guardians can ask us to delete it.

1. Who the Game is for

The Game is intended for a general audience that includes children. We treat every player as a child. This means that we do not ask for the player's age, we do not show personalized advertising to anyone, and we do not use the advertising identifier of the device.

We comply with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • No accounts, passwords or log-in. The Game has no chat, social features or user-generated content.
  • No precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • No Google advertising ID (the Game does not request it).

3. What information is processed

The Game itself stores your progress in files on your device only. The following information is processed automatically by the third-party services built into the Game:

DataPurposeService
Approximate location (country or region, derived from the IP address) Analytics; showing ads Firebase, ad services
Device or app identifiers (not the advertising ID), such as an app-instance ID Analytics; showing and measuring ads; app stability Firebase, ad services
Interactions with the Game (for example, levels started and completed) Analytics: understanding how the Game is played and improving it Firebase Analytics, ad services
Crash logs Finding and fixing crashes Firebase Crashlytics
Diagnostics (device model, operating-system version, app performance) Analytics and app stability Firebase, ad services

This data is not linked to your name or any other directly identifying information. We do not sell it.

4. Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) – Analytics, Crashlytics (crash reports) and Remote Config (game settings). See Firebase privacy information. Collection of the advertising ID is disabled.
  • Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads – display advertising in the Game. See Unity privacy policy. The services are used in child-directed mode.
  • Google Play Billing – processes the optional purchase that removes ads. We receive no payment details; payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

5. Advertising

The Game shows ads. Ads are shown in child-directed mode: they are not personalized, there is no interest-based advertising and no remarketing. Rewarded ads are shown only after the player chooses to watch one. Ads can be removed with an in-app purchase.

6. Notifications

The Game may show reminder notifications. They are created locally on the device and contain no personal data. The Game asks for notification permission only after an adult confirms through the parental gate, and notifications can be turned off at any time in the device settings.

7. Links, purchases and the parental gate

Before the Game opens an external link (for example, e-mail, this policy or the app store page) or starts a purchase, it shows a simple question that is meant to be answered by an adult.

8. Data retention and security

Analytics and crash data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and are deleted automatically after the retention periods configured in our Firebase project . Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Game progress stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

9. Deleting data and parents' rights

Parents and guardians can at any time:

  • Ask us to access or delete the data related to their child's use of the Game, or ask us to stop collecting it. Write to support@dino-logic-games.com with the subject "Delete my data – Last Charge". We will reply within 30 days.
  • Stop all collection by uninstalling the Game. Clearing the app data (Android Settings → Apps → Last Charge → Storage → Clear data) also resets the identifier used by the analytics service.
  • Refuse further collection – tell us that you do not agree, and we will delete the data we can locate.

Because we do not collect names or e-mail addresses, we cannot find data by the name of a person. To help us locate the data, please include in your message the device model, the approximate dates when the Game was used, and, if you can find it, the app-instance ID of the device. Where data cannot be linked to the person making the request, we will explain this in our reply.

Purchases made through Google Play can be managed, and refunds requested, through your Google Play account.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the rights described in the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. You may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features or change service providers. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant, we will say so in the Game's store listing.
