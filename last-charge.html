Privacy Policy for Last Charge

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Last Charge ("the Game"), a mobile game for Android (package com.dino.logic.games.last.charge) published by Dino Logic Games, handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information, and the Game does not require an account. However, third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically collect certain data, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (device model, operating system, language, approximate region)
  • App instance ID and gameplay analytics (session duration, game progress events such as levels started or completed)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • IP address and network information (processed by advertising and analytics services)

2. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its advertising partners

  • Used for displaying banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements
  • May collect Advertising ID, IP address and device information
  • Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config (Google)

  • Used for app analytics, crash reporting and performance monitoring
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

InMobi Choice (consent management)

  • Used to ask for and store your consent choices where required by law
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.inmobi.com/privacy-policy
